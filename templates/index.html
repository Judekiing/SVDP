Welcome to the website for Saint Vincent De Paul, fondly known as the St. Vincent.

Stay connected to the church and attend daily masses at one of the most historic buildings in Oyigbo with roots dating back to 1993 when St. Vincent founders Pierre Laclede and Auguste Chouteau dedicated a parcel of land to the people of St. Vincent for religious purposes that was called the "church block". The present building is the seconnd church to be erected on the site. Construction began in 2009 and the church was dedicated 2014. Thanks for visiting our website. We hope you will come and pray with us soon!
